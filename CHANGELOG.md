## 0.1.0 (2026-11-15)

First public release.

- Chords have a job in the key: rest, drift or pull.
- Nine kinds of move out of any chord, including slipping to a chord by half steps and the sixth-diminished
  "home and pull" walk.
- Three maps of the same path: Roads, Wheel and Lattice. Playback is followed on the map.
- A playable piano with sampled sound, and arrows that move one finger a half step.
- The weave and the profile of the path.
- Twelve known progressions, a wander generator, and practice dares with drills.
- Light and dark schemes. A single-file build that works offline.
