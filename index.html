An improviser's map of harmony

Chord Landscape

Every chord has a job: it rests, it drifts away, or it pulls. Pick a key, tap a chord, and the page shows where you are, what the chord wants, and the moves you can make from it: a whole chord from the list, or one finger at a time on the piano. Each move is drawn on three maps and on the keyboard.

I. The compass

You are here. Where can you go?

Tap any option or any highlighted spot on the map to move there and hear it. Tap a move's name to show only its destinations on the map. Tap a chord in your path to select it; the × beside it removes it, and Undo takes back any change. The piano keys are playable, and the ‹ › arrows beside the piano move one finger (or the bass) a half step: the page names whatever you made and puts it on the maps. Colour is the chord's job: rest, drift, pull.

II. The shape of your path

See what you just played

Two more ways to look at the same chords. The weave shows how each finger moves; flat lines are notes you hold. The profile shows how far each chord sits from rest, so you can see whether your path builds and releases or just circles.
